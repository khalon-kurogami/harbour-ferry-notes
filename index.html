Harbour Ferry: autumn timetable changes

Posted 12 October. These changes take effect on Monday 26 October.

What is changing

  • Route 7 (Pier 2 to the Island) now starts at 5:40 am on weekdays instead of 6:10 am.
  • Pier 4 is closed for repairs until 14 November; Route 3 boards from Pier 5 in the meantime.
  • The last Sunday sailing on Route 7 moves from 9:30 pm to 8:45 pm.
  • Cash fares are unchanged at $4.25; the monthly pass rises from $96 to $99.

Where to get help

Ticket offices stay open 7:00 am to 7:00 pm daily. Lost property is handled at the Pier 2 office.
